# Swara

Fast & fluid native Android music player built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, and Media3.

## Highlights
- **Stream & Search**: Instant search and radio powered by YouTube Music
- **Unlimited Playlist Import**: Import full playlists from YouTube Music & Spotify
- **Offline Downloads**: Download tracks or entire playlists with background progress and custom folder support
- **Lyrics & Romanization**: Synced lyrics with one-tap Hangul/Kana romanization
- **Listen Together**: Real-time synchronized listening rooms with friends
- **Stats & Recap**: Listening streaks, top songs/artists, and shareable Recap Story cards
