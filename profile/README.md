### About Swara

Swara is an independent music player ecosystem crafted with **Kotlin**, **Jetpack Compose**, and **Media3** for Android — designed around fluid animations, clean controls, and zero clutter.

### Core Experience

- **Instant Streaming & Radio** — Smooth audio playback with adaptive quality and smart queue continuation.
- **No-Limit Playlist Import** — Bring over full playlists from YouTube Music and Spotify in their original order.
- **Offline Library & Custom Folder** — Save tracks or entire playlists with chunked background downloads and live progress notifications.
- **Synced & Romanized Lyrics** — Follow along in real time with one-tap Hangul/Kana romanization right on the player screen.
- **Listen Together** — Host or join synchronized listening sessions with friends and see who's tuned in.
- **Listening Stats & Recap** — Track your streaks, top artists, and exportable Story cards.
