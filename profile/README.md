## What we're working on

Development is focused on [Swara](https://github.com/swaramusic/Swara), a native Android YouTube Music client with offline downloads, unlimited playlist import, synchronized & romanized lyrics, Listen Together, and Recap stories.

You can get the latest APK [on our website](https://swaramusic.github.io/Swara/).
